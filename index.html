    FAQ

    Questions business owners ask us.

    What can you automate?

    Common examples: answering WhatsApp FAQs, booking appointments, qualifying leads, handling missed calls, sending reminders and status updates, updating CRMs and spreadsheets, collecting documents, and generating reports. What's feasible depends on the tools you use and the access they allow — we confirm this in the audit.

    Can you connect our current tools?

    Usually, yes. We work with your existing calendars, forms, spreadsheets, CRMs and internal tools wherever they offer a reliable way to connect. For anything less common, we run a short integration discovery first and tell you honestly whether it's practical.

    Will AI replace our staff?

    No. Automation handles routine, repetitive work so your team can focus on the conversations and decisions that need a person. Exceptions and sensitive requests are always handed off to a human.

    How much does it cost?

    It depends on scope. You'll get a one-time build fee for implementation, plus clearly itemized recurring costs — platform, AI model usage, telephony, WhatsApp messaging and any third-party services — and the support plan you choose. No hidden fees.

    How quickly can we launch?

    We give you a timeline once we understand the scope, have access to the tools involved, and know how complex the integrations and testing will be. Starting with a single workflow is the fastest way to go live.

    How do you keep it reliable?

    Testing against real scenarios before launch, input validation, monitoring, automatic retries, detailed logs, and fallback paths if something is unavailable. You'll get alerts and reports, not surprises.

    What happens if the AI is unsure?

    It follows the escalation rules we agree on with you: it can ask a clarifying question, or hand the conversation or call to your team along with a summary of what's been said so far.

    How is our data handled?

    Each workflow only gets the access it needs. We apply data minimization, agreed retention periods and access controls, and we'll tell you which third-party services process your data. Messaging and calls are designed around consent, opt-out and applicable platform and privacy rules.
